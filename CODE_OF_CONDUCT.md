# Code of Conduct

## The short version

I built this for myself, it solved my problem, and I put it up in case it
solves yours too. There is one person here. Be decent and we will get along
fine.

## What that means

Welcome:

- Bug reports, questions, and "am I holding this wrong?"
- Pull requests, including ones that disagree with how I did something.
- Telling me I am wrong, with a reason.

Not welcome:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or bigotry of any flavour — about who someone
  is, where they are from, what they believe, who they love, or what they look
  like.
- Sexualised comments or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Demanding, entitled, or abusive behaviour toward anyone here, me included.
  This is free software maintained in spare time. Nobody owes you a fix, and
  you owe nobody an argument.

## Where this applies

Anywhere the project lives: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, and code review. If you are representing the project somewhere else,
it applies there too.

## Enforcement

There is no committee, no working group, and no appeals process. There is me.

If something needs attention, email <nihaopaul@gmail.com>. Put "Code of
Conduct" in the subject so it does not get lost. I will read it, and I will
keep it to myself.

Depending on what happened, I may edit or delete the offending content, lock a
thread, close a pull request, or block an account from the repository. For
anything serious I will skip straight to blocking. I try to be fair, but these
calls are mine and they are final.

I am one person in one timezone with a day job, so a response may take a while.
That is not indifference — it is arithmetic.

## If you would rather have the formal version

Plenty of projects use the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
and it is a good document. This is not it. This is a smaller promise from a
smaller project, and I would rather write something I can actually stand behind
than adopt language that implies an organisation that does not exist.
